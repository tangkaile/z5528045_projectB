# Task 3 — App, report and submission review

## Goal

Review the Streamlit product, generated results and written report against the Part B brief. Check that the app presents the results clearly, that report values match the generated files and that the final submission contains the required evidence.

## Prompts used

Examples of the requests I gave the AI assistant included:

- Review the Streamlit app against the Part B requirements and identify missing pages, explanations or risk warnings.
- Guide me through testing each app tab one step at a time.
- Check whether the performance values in the report match the generated CSV tables and figures.
- Review the CAST comparison and make sure I do not claim that it improved performance when the results show otherwise.
- Review the submission checklist and identify remaining files or checks required before creating the ZIP.
- Rewrite selected report paragraphs in simpler language without changing their technical meaning.

## What the AI produced

The assistant suggested checks for the Compare Funds, Fact Sheet, Allocation Lab, Sentiment Lens and Methodology pages. It also helped identify where report tables and figures should be placed and explained how to compare the written values with the generated results.

For the final review, it highlighted missing submission items, unnecessary system files and places where the report needed clearer explanations of risk, limitations and the negative CAST result.

## What was useful

The step-by-step app checks helped me confirm that each interactive page loaded and displayed the expected information. The report review also helped me find inconsistencies between wording, table placement and generated results.

The checklist review was useful because it identified technical submission issues that were separate from the quality of the analysis.

## What needed correction or judgement

Some early suggestions were too focused on page count even though my final instruction was to remain below the stated word limit. Some suggested notes and warnings were also repetitive, so I kept only the explanations necessary for interpreting the methodology and results.

I checked the report wording myself and simplified it so that it reflected my own interpretation. I also retained the negative CAST result rather than presenting the innovation as successful.

## Verification performed

I manually tested all five Streamlit tabs and changed the available selections and allocation inputs. I checked the report’s returns, volatility, Sharpe ratios, drawdowns and turnover against the generated CSV results.

I also ran the methodology tests and final hand-in checker, inspected the remaining warnings and removed unnecessary system and cache files before preparing the submission.

## Final responsibility

AI was used to assist with review, troubleshooting and clearer explanations. I selected the final content, checked the numerical evidence and remained responsible for the submitted report, application and interpretation.
